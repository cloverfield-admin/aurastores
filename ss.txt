Lets plan on implementing a subscription model for Aura.

We will use the following plans:

- Free Plan
- Basic Plan
- Pro Plan
- Enterprise Plan

The Free Plan will be free for all users.
The Basic Plan will be ZMW300/month.
The Pro Plan will be ZMW500/month.
The Enterprise Plan will be ZMW1000/month.

# Plan Features
-> Free Plan Features
- Aura Stock Free (Up to 20 Products)
- Sales Tracking (Up to 50 Sales Transactions)
- Product Categories (Up to 20 categories)
- 1 User
- Single Branch Syncing

-> Basic Plan Features
- Aura Stock
- Sales Tracking
- Product Categories (Up to 20 categories)
- 5 Staff Users
- Multi-branch Syncing (Up to 5 branches)

-> Pro Plan Features
- All Basic Plan Features
- Aura Insights
- Multi-branch Syncing (Up to 15 branches)
- 15 Staff Users
- Unlimited Product Categories

-> Enterprise Plan Features
- All Pro Plan Features
- Aura Pay (Accept Mobile Money Payments)
- Multi-branch Syncing (Unlimited branches)
- Unlimited Staff Users

I want to be able to alter the pricing of the plans at any time.
I also want to update the pricing plans on the landing page.
I want users to be able to upgrade and downgrade their plan at any time.
I want users to subscribe monthly, quarterly, and yearly.

create a new role aurapharma_admin.
This role will have all rights and the ability to manage the subscription plans and pricing.
